About DigiBook

Helping education move forward.

DigiBook is an education management platform designed to make everyday administration easier for schools and tuition classes. We bring important student information, staff management and classroom administration into one convenient digital environment.

From student identity cards and timetables to attendance, tuition payments and parent access, our goal is to reduce repetitive paperwork and make educational information easier to manage.

We believe technology should support teachers, simplify administration and help parents stay involved in a student's learning journey.
